Marker color/size (severity), the blue epicenter star, and the spread over time are a documented simulation — no public dataset of real ASEAN base-station GNSS timing under spoofing exists, so this distance-decay spread stands in for "nearby infrastructure sharing correlated timing anomalies." Severity is anchored to the real detector's own predict_proba() range: floor 0.430 (median proba on real held-out clean rows) to ceiling 0.993 (90th-percentile proba on real held-out attack rows) — not invented numbers.
